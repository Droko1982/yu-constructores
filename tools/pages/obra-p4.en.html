Storm and foul sewer network in the La Miranda neighbourhood, Armenia: excavation, pipework, manholes and road reinstatement.

The challenge

Working in an established neighbourhood means trenching streets people use to get home every day. We had to keep pedestrian and vehicle traffic running, maintain permanent signage, and locate existing utility networks that are not always where the drawings say.

Scope of works

  • Locating existing utilities and traffic management
  • Trench excavation with water control
  • Foul and storm pipework installation
  • Manholes and gullies
  • Household connections
  • Layered compacted backfill with density testing
  • Road structure reinstatement
