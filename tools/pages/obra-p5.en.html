Civil and industrial works for a pork processing plant in La Victoria, Valle del Cauca: structure, roofing, technical floors and specialised services.

The challenge

A processing plant is unlike any other building. Floors must withstand constant washing and disinfection without degrading, gradients must carry water to collection points without pooling, and finishes cannot have corners where organic matter accumulates. All of that is settled before pouring, not after.

Scope of works

  • Foundations and structure for industrial use
  • Long-span roofing
  • Technical floors with drainage falls
  • Specialised plumbing and drainage
  • Sanitary wall and junction finishes
  • External works and plant landscaping
