Maintenance and refurbishment of school buildings in Génova, Quindío, including façades and circulation areas.

The challenge

Génova sits in southern Quindío, up in the mountains, and that shapes the logistics: every material run is long and the mountain weather shortens the windows for painting and external work. Purchasing planning and making the most of dry days mattered as much as the building itself.

Scope of works

  • Façade works and painting
  • Circulation area refurbishment
  • Corrective building maintenance
  • Roof and damp repairs
  • Finishes and handover
