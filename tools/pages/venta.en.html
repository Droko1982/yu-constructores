How buying works

  • Information in writing. We send you the exact location on WhatsApp, along with any details you need to make your decision.
  • Viewing the house. We arrange it with you; if you live abroad, a relative or someone holding your power of attorney can go on your behalf.

If you are buying from outside Colombia

A foreigner can buy a home in Colombia with the same rights as a Colombian national. Read the guide for foreigners and, if you live abroad, how the paperwork is signed remotely with a power of attorney, in building in Colombia from abroad.
