Upgrading of school infrastructure at Colegio Libre in Circasia, Quindío: classrooms, roofing and common areas.

The challenge

A school project has a deadline that is not negotiable: the students coming back. The schedule was built around the academic calendar, concentrating noisy work and anything taking areas out of service into the holiday periods.

Scope of works

  • Classroom refurbishment
  • Roof repair and replacement
  • Common area works
  • Electrical services and lighting
  • Painting and finishes
  • Phased handover following the academic calendar
