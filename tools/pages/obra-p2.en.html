Single-family home built in the Malabar estate, Cerritos, Pereira: concrete structure, masonry, services and interior finishes.

The challenge

Building inside a private estate imposes rules an isolated plot does not: delivery windows, truck routes, waste handling and coexistence with neighbours already living there. The schedule was built around those constraints from day one.

Scope of works

  • Foundations and reinforced concrete structure
  • Structural and partition masonry
  • Roofing and waterproofing
  • Plumbing, drainage and electrical services
  • Tiling, flooring and joinery
  • Interior finishes and handover
