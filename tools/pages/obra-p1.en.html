Full construction of a two-storey country house in Alcalá, Valle del Cauca: foundations, structure, roof, swimming pool and finishes.

The challenge

A country house on a slope means solving the ground first and the building second. Before the foundations we had to form the platform, guarantee drainage across the plot and set the access levels — in this region a well-built house on badly prepared ground still ends up cracking.

Scope of works

  • Earthworks and platform forming
  • Foundations and ground slab
  • Two-storey reinforced concrete structure
  • Masonry, rendering and roofing
  • Swimming pool and external works
  • Plumbing, drainage and electrical services
  • Architectural finishes and joinery
