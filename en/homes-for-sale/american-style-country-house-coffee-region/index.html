American-style country house with a stream in Quindío, Colombia

Gated country estate in Quindío, with a footpath along the stream within the plot

  • 427 m²built
  • 2,350 m²plot
  • 4 + 1bedrooms plus staff bedroom
  • 5bathrooms

A spacious single-storey house with American-style architecture, on a 2,350 m² plot with green areas and a footpath that follows the stream within the property itself. It is built with double walls, which insulate it from heat, cold and noise.

Layout

  • Four bedrooms, two of them en-suite.
  • Shared bathroom for the other bedrooms, and a guest WC.
  • Study overlooking the garden.
  • Living and dining room with fireplace and direct access to the terrace.
  • Separate kitchen.
  • Staff bedroom with its own bathroom.
  • Indoor laundry area and a storage room.

Outdoor areas

  • Covered rear terrace, open to the garden.
  • Covered BBQ area.
  • Footpath along the stream within the plot.
  • Dog kennel.
  • Covered parking for two cars.

Water and comfort

  • Reserve tanks of 2,000 litres, with a hydroflow system for water pressure.
  • Double walls with thermal and acoustic insulation.

Running costs

The estate service charge is COP 380,000 a month. We can tell you on WhatsApp what it covers, along with any other details you need to make your decision.

Location

In a gated country estate in Quindío, in the Coffee Region. We will send you the exact location on WhatsApp and arrange the viewing.

If you are buying from outside Colombia

A foreigner can buy a home in Colombia with the same rights as a Colombian national. Before you do, read the guide for foreigners and, if you live abroad, how the paperwork is signed remotely with a power of attorney, in building in Colombia from abroad.

Would you rather build your own?

If none of our houses is the one you are looking for, we will build yours to measure on your own plot: see country houses and fincas or build your request.
